# Privacy Policy — Tidy Bookmarks

**Last updated: 2026-08-04**

Tidy Bookmarks does not collect, transmit, or share any data. Specifically:

- **No network requests.** The extension makes no calls to any server, analytics provider, or third party. Everything it does happens locally inside your browser.
- **Bookmarks permission.** Tidy Bookmarks reads and modifies your Chrome bookmarks (via the `chrome.bookmarks` API) only to display them on the new-tab page and apply actions you take (rename, reorder, archive/collapse). This data never leaves your device.
- **Storage permission.** Tidy Bookmarks stores its own settings and per-folder state (collapsed/archived flags, the favicon-only display preference) locally via `chrome.storage.local`. This data is stored only on your device and is not synced to any external service by the extension.
- **Favicon permission.** Used only to display each bookmark's site icon, fetched through Chrome's built-in favicon cache — no external image requests are made by the extension.
